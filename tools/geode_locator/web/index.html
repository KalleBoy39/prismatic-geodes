About this map

Each marker sits on the feature origin Minecraft uses for the geode. It is exact, computed from your seed with Minecraft's own random number generator. The hollow centre is about 5 blocks further in X, Y and Z.

The terrain is computed from the seed too, with Minecraft's own world generation: biomes, land, water and height. Caves, trees, villages and small inland lakes are not drawn.

Supports worlds of the default type (not Amplified, Large Biomes or Superflat). Chunks that were generated before Prismatic Geodes was installed keep their purple vanilla geodes.
