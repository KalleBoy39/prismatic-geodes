Prismatic Geode Locator 1.0.0
=============================

A map of your Minecraft 26.3 world that shows where every coloured geode from the
Prismatic Geodes mod is, computed from your world seed.

NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT PRODUCT. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT.


START
-----
1. Double-click "Prismatic Geode Locator.exe".
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. First start only: accept the Minecraft EULA. The locator then downloads the official
   Minecraft 26.3 server from Mojang (about 60 MB) and Fabric files from FabricMC.
   You need internet for this once.
3. The map opens in your browser. Keep the small locator window open while you use it.
   Close that window to stop the locator.


USE
---
- In Minecraft, type /seed in chat. Paste the number into "World seed" and click "Show".
- Drag to move, scroll to zoom. Click a colour to hide or show it.
- Click a crystal for its coordinates, "Copy coordinates" or "Copy /tp command".
- "Verify visible area" checks which geodes really formed (terrain stops about 1 in 10).
  It builds that area in a hidden test world on this PC. The first time takes a few minutes.

Works for worlds of the default type with Prismatic Geodes 1.0.0. Areas explored before
the mod was installed keep their purple vanilla geodes.


FILES
-----
Everything the locator downloads or creates is in the "data" folder next to this file.
It never opens or changes your real Minecraft worlds.
To uninstall, close the locator and delete this folder.
